## Skills Salary Analysis

### Project Overview
Analysed the relationship between in-demand skills and their median salaries across different job roles and countries to help job seekers make informed career decisions.

### How I Created It
1. **Data Preparation:** Cleaned and transformed the raw dataset using Power Query.
2. **Data Modelling:** Created relationships and calculated measures using Power Pivot and DAX.
3. **Pivot Table Analysis:** Summarised skills, median salaries and skills likelihood.
4. **Data Visualization:** Created a combination chart to compare median salaries and skills likelihood.
5. **Interactive Filtering:** Added Job Title and Job Country slicers with multi-select functionality.

### Business Value for Job Seekers
- Identify skills associated with higher salaries.
- Understand the demand for different technical skills.
- Prioritize learning based on salary potential and market demand.
- Make informed career development decisions.

### Final Outcome
Developed an interactive Excel analysis that helps job seekers explore skill demand and salary trends across different job roles and countries.

## Skills, Job & Country Analysis

### Project Overview
Analysed the most frequently required skills across different job titles and countries to help job seekers understand which technical skills are in demand.

### How I Created It
1. **Data Preparation:** Cleaned and transformed the dataset using Power Query.
2. **Data Analysis:** Used Pivot Tables to calculate the number of job postings associated with each skill.
3. **Data Visualization:** Created a column chart to display the Top 10 most in-demand skills.
4. **Interactive Filtering:** Added Job Title and Job Country slicers to explore skill demand across different roles and countries.

### Business Value for Job Seekers
- Identify the most frequently required technical skills.
- Understand skill requirements across different job roles.
- Explore skill demand across countries.
- Prioritize learning based on job market demand.

### Final Outcome
Created an interactive Excel analysis that helps job seekers identify in-demand skills and understand how skill requirements vary across job titles and countries.
